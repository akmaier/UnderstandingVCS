\subsection{The neuroscience analyses}\label{sec:results-a}

We begin with a known concern in the field. Jonas and Kording asked whether a
neuroscientist with the standard toolkit could understand a microprocessor. They
found that the toolkit yields rich structure but misses the
mechanism~\cite{jonas2017could}. They could show the failure but not measure it.
On the 6502 (the CPU model) they had no causal ground truth to score each method against. We
supply that missing number. We run the same analyses on the bit-exact, fully
known VCS, where exact intervention recovers the true causal role of every
register and RAM cell (\Sectionname\ref{sec:methods}). This makes Kording's
qualitative lesson quantitative. Across the eight analyses A1--A8,
faithfulness against the oracle ranges from $0.14$ to $1.0$ and averages
$0.46$~\cite{leaderboardP2}. The
oracle-as-method positive
control sits at $1.0$ by construction (Table~\ref{tab:phaseA}). The structure
these methods report is real. But structure is not the same as cause. The score
measures the difference.

The dependency structure is only partly recoverable. A1 reconstructs the dependency
graph over state variables, a connectome of cell-to-cell edges found by single-shot
perturbation, and scores it against the true data-flow graph at a faithfulness of
$0.21$~\cite{leaderboardP2}. Pooled over the scored games, $42$ of the $56$ edges it
reports are real, but it finds only $42$ of the $387$ true edges. The single-unit
lesion A2 fares best. It clamps each RAM cell in turn and ranks it by the
behavioral damage it does. The resulting importance map has a Spearman
correlation of about $1.0$ with the unit's true causal role on the six core games (listed in \Sectionname\ref{sec:results-b}),
and averages $0.97$ over the scored games~\cite{leaderboardP2}. Lesioning is causal,
which is why A2 is the one classical method that scores near the top.

Yet a near-perfect rank order still misses interactions. A single-unit lesion
changes one cell at a time, so it is blind to causes that act only together. On
Space Invaders the per-unit ranking misses $15\%$ of the pairwise interactions the
oracle records ($8$ of $55$ pairs; $19\%$ averaged over the scored games). One
super-additive pair moves the importance score by $40$ beyond the sum of its
parts~\cite{leaderboardP2}. The ranking of which units
matter is correct, but the model of how they combine is missing. So even the
strongest classical method recovers a ranking, not the computation. An effect
map does not show the algorithm behind it.

Tuning curves report structure that does not match the true cause. A3 fits
per-unit tuning to a game variable, the workhorse of systems neuroscience. On the
VCS it actively misleads. Its \emph{spurious-tuning rate} is the fraction of
strongly-tuned units whose tuning does not match their true causal role. That
rate is $0.50$ on Pong. Its faithfulness, the rank correlation between tuning
selectivity and the oracle's causal importance, averages $0.21$, among the lowest of
the analyses~\cite{leaderboardP2}. Tuning is correlational. On a machine where many
cells co-vary with the frame clock, apparent tuning is cheap. This is the same
present-versus-used distinction we discuss later for linear probing.

Correlation structure and field potentials are real, but they are mostly side
effects, not causes. A4, the spike-word analysis (which cells fire together) of pairwise and global
correlation, reproduces the familiar weak-pairwise/strong-global signature on $23$ of its $33$
scored games. Yet
against the true coupling its faithfulness is only $0.23$, with low sufficiency
($S = 0.14$; $S$ is a held-out predictive fit, which can be negative per game and is clipped to $[0,1]$ before averaging)~\cite{leaderboardP2}. A5 is the local-field-potential (voltage from nearby neurons)
analogue that pools regional activity into power spectra. It scores $0.38$, and
much of that variance is the known clocks, the frame counter and the free-running
timer cells~\cite{leaderboardP2}. Both methods detect the machine's activity but not
its computation.

Granger causality treats ``happens earlier'' as ``is the cause.'' A6 infers
directed edges between RAM cells and between the CPU, TIA, and RIOT (the TIA is the Television Interface Adaptor, the graphics/sound chip, and the RIOT the RAM-I/O-Timer). Its faithfulness is the $F_1$ of the inferred cell-to-cell edges against the true data-flow. On Pong
its faithfulness is $0.0$, with false-edge and missed-edge rates both at
$1.0$~\cite{leaderboardP2}. The VCS is clock-locked, so nearly everything is
predictable from everything else one cycle earlier. Equating precedence with
causation therefore infers mostly spurious edges: averaged over the scored games,
$78\%$ of the inferred edges are false and $78\%$ of the true edges are missed. We
ran one setting (lag $2$, $\alpha=0.05$) and did not sweep lag or threshold.
Precedence and causation differ here, and only the intervention oracle separates
them.

Dimensionality reduction scores in the middle. A7 decomposes the state tensor with
NMF (non-negative matrix factorization) and PCA (principal component analysis) and
matches the top components to the known game variables and the known clock and
read/write signals. Its score is the matched fraction, the share of the top five
components that align with a known variable or signal. On the core games both
factorizations match about 60 percent (NMF $0.60$, PCA $0.57$). Over the scored
games NMF averages $0.52$ and PCA only $0.35$; the score we report is
NMF's~\cite{leaderboardP2}. This makes A7 the highest score after the causal lesion
and the trivial whole-state record. It still leaves about half of the components
unmatched.

A complete record is still not an explanation. A8 records the full RAM and
register state, faithful and sufficient by definition ($F = 1.0$, $S = 1.0$; faithfulness $F\in[0,1]$ runs from chance at $0$ to the oracle ceiling at $1$).
Yet its minimality is only $0.08$ ($M\in[0,1]$)~\cite{leaderboardP2}. At the algorithmic level,
a recording of every cell explains nothing. Faithfulness and sufficiency without
minimality give a log file, not an account of the computation. This is the first
sign that the three axes are independent. The semantic-gap result makes that
point central later.

Taken together, these analyses confirm the expected result and puts a number on
it. The advance over Jonas and Kording is that we grade the \emph{architectural}
register-transfer level (registers and the logic between them) with a \emph{quantitative} $F$ against an exact oracle,
rather than giving a qualitative verdict on the transistor. The contrast is
clear, because the causal operators we apply to the same machine in
\Sectionname\ref{sec:results-b} and~\Sectionname\ref{sec:results-c} reach the ceiling these analyses cannot. So the limitation lies in the analysis methods, not in the machine. We
mean this strictly as calibration. Even the best of these analyses recover a
ranking, a graph, or a record, never the algorithm the program runs. This
remaining gap is what the paper measures.
